With Grain mix and Hall mix at 0, or in FX Bypass, the regular Toy Piano sound comes out unchanged.

3. Playing

  • S1–S8 play C4–C5, S9–S16 C5–C6 (white keys). Each press starts a one-shot attack/decay envelope; releasing does not stop it. LEDs D1–D16 show the held keys.
  • The keys send their notes over USB-MIDI on channel 1. Incoming MIDI notes (any channel, with velocity) play 16 more voices.

5. Controls

SW17 only selects which page the eight pots edit. Both the piano and the effects keep running with their settings.

SwitchFunction
SW18 upFreeze: the recording stops and the grains keep playing the held sound; the reverb keeps running. Releasing cross-fades back to live input.
SW18 centreRun: normal operation
SW18 downFX Bypass: new sound no longer enters the effects, the tail rings out for about 2 s, then the output is the exact dry piano
Which way is “up” on your kit is checked in the first board run; please note it.
  • Pickup: after a page change, a MIDI CC or an editor change, a pot does nothing until you turn it to the stored value (or across it). Values never jump.
  • Power-up: with SW17 off, the Piano page starts at the pot positions. The FX page always starts at gentle defaults (little grain and reverb mix) and picks up as described.

6. Editor and modulation

  • Use the Mod on Daisy editor (Daisy Control Panel / VST3 0.5.1 or the browser editor 0.4.1); the instrument reports as toy-piano-cumulonimbus. Close other programs that hold the MIDI port.
  • Matrix targets: Pitch, Level, Harmonic, Attack, Decay, Grain position, Grain mix, Hall mix. VR6–VR8 (Piano page) set the patch's LFO and Random rates; an idle editor shows the change within about 2 s.
